# Wefilling | Weekly To-do and Campus Guide Copy

Source: the 161-page Korean, Chinese and English ERICA Fall 2026 orientation booklet. The 16-week structure follows the existing app. Placement of general guides is an editorial recommendation, not a university deadline. Dated items include the actual period in their text. Prepared on 2026-09-21; weeks 1–3 are historical guidance. University schedules may change. Complete actual applications through the university or relevant institution.

## Week 1 · 2026-09-01 ~ 2026-09-06

### Check your HY-in access

Open HY-in through the university website and check that you can sign in. After your first sign-in, change the initial password following the university instructions. If you have already changed it, simply confirm that you can access your account.

- Audience: International and Korean students (subject to conditions in the text)
- Checkable guide (`required`) · ID: `portal_access`
- Source: PDF 27 / KO 23
- Link: [link](https://portal.hanyang.ac.kr/)

For students covered by the ERICA Fall 2026 undergraduate course adjustment schedule. The booklet lists 2026.09.03~09.08 as the final adjustment period. Review your courses and timetable in HY-in and follow the detailed university notice if changes are needed. Graduate and exchange students should check applicability and the exact cutoff for their own program. After the period ends, use this as a record of the semester schedule.

- Audience: International and Korean students (subject to conditions in the text)
- Reference information (`notice`) · ID: `course_correction`
- Source: PDF 5, 27, 28 / KO 1, 23, 24
- Link: [link](https://portal.hanyang.ac.kr/)

For students who need foreign resident registration or other residence guidance after arrival. Check your status and period of stay, then consult OIA and HiKorea about applications, documents and appointments. Applicable deadlines depend on your arrival date and circumstances; do not wait for the week in which this guide appears. Contact the Immigration Contact Center at 1345 if needed.

- Audience: International students (subject to conditions in the text)
- Reference information (`notice`) · ID: `residence_start`
- Source: PDF 7, 9, 10, 11 / KO 3, 5, 6, 7

## Week 2 · 2026-09-07 ~ 2026-09-13

### Set up your mobile student ID

For eligible students who have not set up a mobile student ID. Follow the university instructions in Hey Young Campus (헤이영 캠퍼스) to verify your identity and obtain the mobile ID. If you need a physical card, first obtain the mobile ID and then check the student ID application guidance in HY-in. Contact ERICA Student Support at 031-400-4307.

- Audience: International and Korean students (subject to conditions in the text)
- Suggested activity (`recommendation`) · ID: `mobile_student_id`
- Source: PDF 21, 22 / KO 17, 18
- Link: [link](https://portal.hanyang.ac.kr/)

### Check your insurance and payment notices

University group insurance and National Health Insurance are separate systems. Ask OIA whether you are covered by the university policy. If you receive a National Health Insurance bill, check your own amount and payment deadline. Keep receipts after treatment and ask the insurer which documents are needed if you wish to make a claim. Eligibility, premiums and coverage are not identical for all students.

- Audience: International students (subject to conditions in the text)
- Reference information (`notice`) · ID: `insurance_guidance`
- Source: PDF 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 / KO 7, 8, 9, 10, 11
- Link: [link](https://global.hanyang.ac.kr/)

### Check required training and Korean courses

Check OIA email for eligibility, dates and instructions for laws-and-regulations training and human-rights/violence-prevention training. The booklet lists the former once per semester and the latter once per year. New students admitted through international admission should also check whether intensive Korean courses apply or an exemption is available, and read the OIA class assignment email. Follow the separate notices and your registered timetable for deadlines and class times.

- Audience: International students (subject to conditions in the text)
- Reference information (`notice`) · ID: `mandatory_education`
- Source: PDF 28, 29 / KO 24, 25
- Link: [link](https://global.hanyang.ac.kr/)
